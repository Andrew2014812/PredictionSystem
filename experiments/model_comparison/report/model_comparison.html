FootPredict v5 — comparison of alternative ML models

Research report, not part of the website. Generated from experiments/model_comparison. Nothing here changes the production models.

Summary

  • Match result (1X2): CatBoost (best mean, but not clearly better than XGBoost)
  • Home goals: LightGBM (clearly better than XGBoost)
  • Away goals: Poisson regression (best mean, but not clearly better than XGBoost)
  • Total corners: Poisson regression (best mean, but not clearly better than XGBoost)
  • BTTS: derived · GLM (mean log loss 0.6876; next derived · LightGBM 0.6879)
  • Totals (over 2.5): derived · Ensemble (mean log loss 0.6806; next derived · Improved XGBoost 0.6809)

Results that are close or not significant are reported as such; no experimental model replaces the FootPredict production model without a separate decision.

Method

  • Data: Football-Data results and statistics of 21 leagues, the same leak-free pre-match features as FootPredict (each match only sees matches played before its day).
  • Walk-forward folds: for an evaluated season s, every algorithm is tuned on train = seasons before s−1 and validation = s−1, then refitted on all seasons before s and scored on s. Identical rows, targets, features (one-hot league; linear models additionally median-imputed and standardised with missing-value flags) and metrics for every algorithm.
  • Winners are chosen on the folds 2023/24, 2024/25. 2025/26 was the test season of v1–v4 and has been looked at; it is reported but not used to choose.
  • Differences are tested with a paired bootstrap over matches (95 % interval of the mean per-match loss difference against the current XGBoost). A model is called better only when the whole interval is on the good side.
  • ROI uses real Bet365 prices (Football-Data) only, the same matches and the same rule for every model; such samples are small relative to the noise of betting returns.
